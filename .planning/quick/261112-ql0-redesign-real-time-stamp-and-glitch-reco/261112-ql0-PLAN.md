<objective>
Redesign the real-time stamp and glitch recording to close the three review findings deferred from Phase 01 iteration 3: WR-03 (index-paired stamps and ring wrap; the maintainer ranks it Critical), WR-01 (racy point-based overload rejection) and IN-08 (unchecked mFlags).

Purpose: SoundPing exists to produce latency numbers with no hidden offset. Today a single skipped stamp silently shifts the round trip and the stored calibration by a whole buffer, and a glitch can slip past the rejection check. After this change a stamp can only be paired with its own callback, and a hit is rejected whenever any recorded fault interval touches it. The fault check reads backend state directly, never the event stream.

Output:
- A paired stamp ring and fault-interval rings in RTState, written by one `@_noLocks` `ioCycle` entry point.
- `AudioBackend.faults(overlappingInput:output:)`, implemented by the HAL and simulated backends and used by HitListener.
- A range-safe clock self-test.
- Tests.
- The updated 01-REVIEW-FIX.md.
</objective>

     - Doc: it returns every recorded fault whose frames overlap the input range (input faults) or the output range (output faults).

   - Keep four instances, each with exactly one writer:

     - **Output faults:** written by the IOProc of the output direction. On a shared device one IOProc writes both of these.
     - **Input overloads and output overloads:** written only from DeviceListeners' serial queue.

     - **Fail safe on wrap.** Once a ring has overwritten entries, a query range for that ring's direction that starts below the lowest lower bound among the ring's retained entries also gets one logOverwritten fault.
